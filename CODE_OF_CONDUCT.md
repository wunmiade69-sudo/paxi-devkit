# Code of Conduct

PAXI DevKit contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate professionally and respectfully. Discussions should focus on the code, documentation, evidence, and user impact rather than on people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, sexualized attention, and disruptive conduct in issues, pull requests, reviews, chats, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit, close, or remove material that violates this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact path in `SECURITY.md` rather than escalating them in a public issue.
